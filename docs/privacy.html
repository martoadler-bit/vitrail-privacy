Vitrail Privacy Policy

Vitrail generates audio on your device. The app and its AUv3 extension do not collect personal data, track your activity, or include advertising or analytics services.

Your music

The standalone app stores its current patch on your device. Exported patches and audio are saved where you choose. When you use the AUv3 instrument, the host can store the patch in its own project. Files you share are handled by the destination you select.

Support

If you email support, your email address and the information you send are used to respond to your request.
